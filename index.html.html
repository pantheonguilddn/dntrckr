❔ Pantheon Tracker Help

Use the tabs on the left to learn the part of the tracker you are currently using. You can also click the small ? buttons beside controls throughout the tool.

Getting Started

Start by choosing your identity from 👤 Who are you?. This tells the tracker which roster belongs to you and enables Personal View.

Simple rule: select your identity once, then use the tracker normally. Your identity is saved in your browser.

Universal View is for seeing the guild-wide board. Personal View is for focusing on your own roster.

Tracker Registration

Use Tracker Registration to opt characters into progress tracking under a member/owner from Member Roster. Characters registered here become available to both the tracker and Team Builder.

1. Choose an existing owner, or choose ＋ Create new group.
2. Enter the exact in-game character name.
3. Select the character's Class / Job.
4. For a new group, choose the owner's name and a color.
5. Press Register Character.

You can later rename groups, change colors, remove characters, or drag characters into a different order.

Guild-wide setting: roster changes affect everyone. Only change roster structure when it is actually needed.

Dungeon Registration

Use Dungeon Registration to maintain the tasks displayed on the tracker.

1. Choose an existing category or create a new one.
2. Choose whether the category is Daily or Weekly.
3. Choose a pastel color for a new category.
4. Enter the exact task name and register it.

Existing categories can be renamed, recolored, switched between Daily/Weekly, reordered, or removed. Tasks can also be dragged to reorder them.

Important: Daily/Weekly classification affects reset behavior and weekly completion calculations, so this is an administrative feature.

Universal View vs Personal View

🌍 Universal View shows the active rosters together. Use it when organizing parties or checking guild-wide progress.

👤 Personal View filters the board to the roster associated with your selected identity.

Nothing is copied or separated. Personal View is only a filter on the same shared data.

To switch, click the view button. Universal and Personal View can be toggled back and forth at any time.

Tracker & Bounty Checker

Use the checkbox to record a completed task. Uncheck it if it was marked by mistake. The weekly reset archives progress, resets weekly states, and clears bounty target text for a clean new week.

Bounty workflow

For a dungeon you want to use for a Unique+ bounty, right-click the dungeon cell. It becomes 🎯 Marked and the system places that dungeon into the first available bounty target slot.

When you later complete the marked dungeon, checking it can automatically complete the linked bounty slot.

Right-click states

Right-click cycles through the special states: Open → 🎯 Marked → 🔄 Reset → N/A → Open.

N/A means the task does not apply to that character. Bounty Claim slots are tracked separately and are not included in weekly completion rate; N/A tasks are also excluded.

Easy rule: left-click to record completion. Right-click when you need to mark, reset, or exclude a task.

Roster Analytics

Weekly analytics is opt-in. When Track weekly analytics is unchecked, normal daily and weekly task resets still run, but new historical snapshots are not saved. This switch is shared across connected clients.

When enabled, the tracker retains up to five weekly snapshots to support the visible trend and priority calculations. Use Clear Stored History to remove old snapshots and reduce the shared cloud record size.

5-Week Trend

Shows the most recent weekly completion percentages available for that character.

Loot-Weighted Priorities

The system looks at missed dungeons, how often they were missed, N/A history, and the configured loot weight. It then surfaces up to three priority dungeons.

Think of it as a planning aid. It is there to highlight what may be worth completing, not to replace the actual tracker.

Loot Weights

Every weekly task can be assigned a weight from 1 to 5:

1 = Minor · 2 = Low · 3 = Medium · 4 = High · 5 = Critical

These weights affect how strongly missed content contributes to the Analytics priority score.

Open Loot Weights, choose the desired number for each task, then press Save Weights. Reset Defaults restores the predefined settings.

Weights do not change completion. They only change how Analytics evaluates missed content.

Card Database

Card Database is a local reference tool. Its filters and personal card batches stay in this browser and never add traffic or data to the guild's shared tracker database.

Filter cards for a build

Choose Level 70 and below, Level 50 - 60, or Level 40 - 50. Select Magic, Physical, or Defensive to set a starter group of stats, or check stats manually. All selected means every chosen stat must be on the same card; Any selected only needs one.

The Defensive group contains Defense, Magic Def, Vit, and Max HP. Choose Magic, Rare, Epic, Unique, or Legend to preview those values. Click the overlapping-squares icon beside a card name to copy it for the in-game Trading House, or open the card for its full five-rarity table.

Import future card updates

Click Manage Card Batches, name the update, and paste a JSON array. Every card requires a unique number, name, nest or pool label, a valid level cap (40, 50, 60, or 70), and stats. Each stat requires five numeric values in Magic, Rare, Epic, Unique, Legend order. Export your local batches as a backup before changing browsers or clearing site data.

Member Roster

Use Member Roster as the guild's member directory. Add people whether or not they use the tracker. Existing registered owners are shown automatically, alongside anyone added to the directory.

How it connects

Add or maintain the member/owner here first. Use Add character on a member card to add a directory-only character or alias without automatically adding it to Tracker or Team Builder. Each listed character has its own × button.

Character chips marked DIRECTORY are unregistered names; removing one only removes that alias. Chips marked TRACKING represent a registered character; removing one from this page also unregisters it from Tracker and Team Builder and deletes its Job assignment, while keeping the member in Member Roster. If you unregister a character in Tracker Registration instead, the character stays in Member Roster as a directory-only entry. To opt it in again, use Tracker Registration and choose the appropriate owner.

When Cloud is configured, member names, aliases, and notes are shared with the guild. Card Database data and card batches are not part of that sync.

Team Builder

Team Builder is the second page of the tracker. It uses the characters registered in Tracker Registration.

Set each character's Job

Open Configure Classes and choose the correct Job. The Class, Raid Position, and default DPS side are filled automatically from the guild's Job list. The Class dropdown can also be used first to narrow the character list.

Choose a Party Format

4 Man Normal is for casual normal nests. 4 Man Hardcore is for higher-intensity 4-man content such as Hell Nests and Dragon Memoria. 8 Man Normal is for Classic Raids with more composition flexibility. 8 Man Hardcore uses both teams for high-end mechanics and exposes physical / magical DPS balance.

Build the two teams

Select characters in Team 1 or Team 2. The builder fills in their Job, Raid Position, DPS side, and Owner automatically. Use Role Settings to adjust the underlying class, primary position, DPS type, and lower-priority secondary roles when the class balance changes.

Team Builder is local to your PC. Building, clearing, or changing teams does not sync to the guild database and will not create cloud traffic. Use 📸 Share Setup to copy the complete two-team setup as an image for Discord.

Character duplicates are always prevented. Allow same owner lets one player contribute different characters to coordinated parties when needed.

Split Party Mechanic

For 8 Man Normal and 8 Man Hardcore, enable Split Party Mechanic when Team 1 and Team 2 must function as separate parties. The builder then checks each 4-man team separately. In 8 Man Hardcore, it also enables the physical / magical DPS balance panel and the deeper per-team utility checks.

Turn it off when the raid does not split the party. The builder then treats both teams as one 8-man composition and only checks the normal baseline of Heal, Tank / Provoke, and DPS coverage.

Shared data: Roster and class information is part of the shared database, so keep character details accurate. 8 Man Hardcore with Split Party Mechanic enabled also shows deeper composition checks such as missing Heal, Tank / Provoke, Debuff, Mobility, Beyond Time, Highlander, and low DPS coverage.

Version History

Use 🕘 Version History to see protected global changes. You can also press Push Version to mark a savepoint.

The tracker keeps a compact shared revision journal in the normal database. The tracker versions only shared, global-impact changes such as roster / character structure, dungeon structure, Job / Role settings, loot weights, and the shared split-party setting. Routine tracker checkmarks, notes, salary logs, and Team Builder slot assignments do not create versions. The PC that makes a supported global change records the revision; refreshing another PC does not create a duplicate or fake revision. When you restore one, the current live state is saved first as a safety version, then the selected version becomes the new live state.

Best practice: push a version before large cleanups. Example: “Before roster cleanup · Oct 4”.

Salary Calculator

Use 🧮 Salary Calc when splitting party income.

Enter the Total Party Income, Party Size, Total Seal Stamps Used, Stamp Comp. Rate, and Salary Holder Cut.

The calculator shows:

Gross Income − Salary Holder Cut − Seal Stamp Cost = Net Income Pool

The Net Income Pool is then divided by the party size to show the Payout per member.

Tip: enter the actual numbers from the party run and use the displayed payout. The calculator updates automatically as you type.

The revision journal is stored in a separate small JSONBin so the main tracker database can never be bloated or blocked by version history. Routine checkmarks, notes, salary logs, and Team Builder slot edits do not create revisions.
