☁️ JSONBin Cloud Setup

🎁 Loot Gravity Weights

Weight 5 means missing the dungeon's loot is critical; weight 1 means minor. Priority also considers missed rate, number of misses, and historical N/A rate. Frequently N/A'd dungeons are automatically deprioritized instead of being counted as misses.
